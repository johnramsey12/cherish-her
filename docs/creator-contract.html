Cherish Her

Independent Contractor Agreement

Please read this agreement carefully before joining the Cherish Her Creator Program.

1. Parties and Relationship

This Independent Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Cherish Her ("Company") and the creator ("Contractor") who submits this agreement.

The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venture of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement shall create an employment relationship between the parties.

2. Services

The Contractor agrees to promote the Cherish Her mobile application through their social media channels and content using their assigned creator code. The Contractor has full creative control over how they promote the app, provided content is truthful and complies with FTC disclosure requirements.

3. Commission and Payment

  • Contractor earns 3% of the sale value of purchases attributed to their creator code within the attribution window
  • Creator commission shall never exceed 90% of the Company's net commission earned on any single sale
  • Attribution window: 48 hours for Amazon purchases, 30 days for other networks
  • Minimum payout threshold: $50 per month
  • Payouts are processed on the second Monday of each month for the previous month's confirmed earnings
  • Payments are sent via Stripe direct deposit to the bank account on file
  • Earnings below $50 in a given month roll over to the next month

4. Tax Responsibilities

The Contractor is solely responsible for all taxes on their earnings, including but not limited to federal income tax, state income tax, and self-employment tax.

The Company will not withhold any taxes from payments made to the Contractor. The Contractor acknowledges they are responsible for making estimated tax payments as required by law.

The Company will issue a Form 1099-NEC to any Contractor who earns $600 or more in a calendar year, as required by US tax law. Contractors are responsible for providing accurate tax information.

The Contractor agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Company from any claims, liabilities, or penalties arising from the Contractor's failure to pay taxes on their earnings.

5. FTC Disclosure Requirements

Contractor agrees to comply with all FTC guidelines regarding material connections. When promoting Cherish Her, Contractor must clearly disclose their affiliate relationship using language such as "#ad", "#sponsored", or "I earn a commission when you use my code."

6. Intellectual Property

The Contractor retains ownership of all content they create. The Company retains ownership of the Cherish Her brand, app, and platform. The Contractor is granted a limited, non-exclusive license to use the Cherish Her name and logo solely for the purpose of promoting the app.

7. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time with 30 days written notice. The Company may terminate immediately for violation of this Agreement, illegal activity, or conduct that damages the Company's reputation.

Upon termination, any pending earnings above the minimum threshold will be paid on the next scheduled payout date.

8. Confidentiality

The Contractor agrees to keep confidential any non-public information about the Company's business, commission rates, conversion data, or affiliate relationships disclosed during this agreement.

9. Limitation of Liability

The Company's total liability to the Contractor shall not exceed the total commissions paid to the Contractor in the three months preceding the claim.

10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Alabama, without regard to conflict of law principles.

11. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter herein and supersedes all prior agreements. Modifications must be in writing and signed by both parties.
