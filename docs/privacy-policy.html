Privacy Policy

Cherish Her  ·  Last updated: June 2026

The short version: Cherish Her stores everything — your partner's profile, preferences, and gift history — entirely on your device. We never collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. No account is required.

1. Information We Collect

Cherish Her is a local-first app. All data you enter — including your partner's name, birthday, anniversary, style preferences, and gift history — is stored using your device's local SQLite database.

We do not operate any servers that receive personal data from the app. We do not create user accounts or require login.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device and never transmitted to us:

  • Partner profile information (name, birthday, anniversary dates)
  • Style, gift, and date preferences from in-app surveys
  • Saved and liked products
  • Notification reminder settings

You can permanently delete all stored data at any time via Settings → Reset All Data.

3. Optional AI Features

Cherish Her includes optional AI-powered explanations powered by the Anthropic Claude API. This feature is disabled by default and only activated if you voluntarily enter your own Anthropic API key in Settings.

If you choose to use this feature, product information (name, description, category) is sent to Anthropic's API to generate personalized explanations. No personal information about you or your partner is included in these requests. Please review Anthropic's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle API data.

4. Affiliate Links

Product recommendations in Cherish Her include affiliate links to third-party retailers. When you tap "Shop on [Retailer]," you are redirected to that retailer's website in your browser. We may earn a commission on qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you. These third-party websites have their own privacy policies.

5. Push Notifications

Cherish Her can send local push notifications for birthdays, anniversaries, and date-night reminders. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device — no notification data is sent to any server. You can disable notifications at any time in the app's Settings or your device's notification settings.

6. Analytics & Crash Reporting

Cherish Her does not use third-party analytics services. We do not track app usage, user behavior, or crashes through any external service.

7. Children's Privacy

Cherish Her is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
