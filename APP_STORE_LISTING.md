## APP DESCRIPTION  (max 4000 chars — paste exactly as-is)

Never wonder what to give her again.

Cherish Her is the personal gift advisor that learns exactly what the special woman in your life loves — then helps you find the perfect gift, plan the perfect date, and never miss an important occasion.

──────────────────────────

🎁 GIFT RECOMMENDATIONS

Browse hundreds of curated products across jewelry, skincare, fashion, experiences, home décor, tech, and more. Every recommendation is scored against her personal style, your budget, and the occasion — so you always find something she'll actually love.

Filter by occasion — birthday, anniversary, Valentine's Day, Christmas, Mother's Day, graduation, and more. Sort by budget, trending, or best match. Tap any product to see why it's a great pick and shop directly.

──────────────────────────

💕 DATE NIGHT IDEAS

Discover restaurant picks and activity ideas curated for the two of you. Whether you want a cozy dinner, an outdoor adventure, a spa day, or a cultural experience — get complete date plans that match her interests and your budget.

──────────────────────────

📅 NEVER MISS A MOMENT

Set personalized reminders for her birthday, your anniversary, Valentine's Day, Christmas, and more — days, weeks, or a month in advance. Cherish Her keeps you prepared so every occasion feels intentional.

──────────────────────────

✨ AI-POWERED MATCHING

Our recommendation engine analyzes her style preferences, favorite categories, budget range, and interests to surface the most relevant suggestions first. Optional AI explanations (powered by Claude) explain exactly why each gift is a perfect match.

──────────────────────────

🔒 COMPLETELY PRIVATE

Everything stays on your phone. Your partner's profile, your preferences, your gift history — all stored locally on your device. No account required. No data ever leaves your phone.

──────────────────────────

PERFECT FOR:
• Boyfriends and husbands planning the perfect gift
• Anyone who wants to feel more thoughtful and intentional
• People who love giving but hate the guesswork
• Last-minute gifting emergencies (Emergency Mode saves the day)

Download Cherish Her and never give a generic gift again.

## APP REVIEW NOTES (paste into "Notes for App Review" field)

Cherish Her is a gift and date recommendation app for partners and couples.

Key notes for reviewers:
• No account or login required — app is fully functional immediately on first launch
• All user data is stored locally on-device using SQLite (expo-sqlite)
• External links are affiliate links to retailers (Amazon, Nordstrom, etc.) — users are shown "Affiliate link" disclosure before tapping
• The AI feature (Settings → AI Features) is optional and disabled by default; requires user's own Anthropic API key
• Push notifications are local only — no server-side push infrastructure
• To test the full flow: complete the Profile Survey (tap "Set up profile" on Home screen), then browse Gifts and Date Ideas

Test account: Not required — no login exists.
